· · about

A short story

I'm a theoretical physicist by training, and my research lives in some challenging corners of quantum theory. This includes instantons and other nonperturbative effects, semiclassical descriptions, and quantum tunneling. In short, the kinds of calculations that keep the path integral interesting.

Along the way, almost any topic in high energy physics can catch my attention, especially the problems where careful thinking pays off. That curiosity doesn't stop at physics, though. I'm just as happy tinkering with new technologies, and learning across disciplines.
